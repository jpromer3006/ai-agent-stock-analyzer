## Portfolio & Business Overview

**Structure & Heritage**
Lamar converted to REIT status in 2014 and reorganized as an UPREIT in 2022 [1]. Founded in 1902 and publicly traded on NASDAQ as "LAMR" since 1996, the company operates three segments: **billboard, logo signs, and transit advertising** [1].

**Billboard Portfolio — Scale & Density**
- ~**159,300 billboard advertising displays** in 45 states and Canada [1]
- ~**5,500 digital billboard advertising displays** in 43 states and Canada [1]
- ~**40,600 transit advertising displays** in 23 states and Canada [1]
- Bulletin rentals account for approximately **77%** of billboard net revenues; poster rentals approximately **23%** [1]

**Logo Signs — Dominant Market Position**
Lamar is the **largest provider of logo signs in the United States**, operating 24 of the 28 privatized state logo sign contracts [1]. This creates a powerful, near-monopolistic position in a niche with high barriers to entry.

**Revenue Mix & Customer Profile**
- Local advertising constituted approximately **79%** of outdoor net revenues for FY2025, which management notes is higher than the industry average [1]
- No single-customer concentration data was surfaced in the 10-K search — consistent with LAMR's highly diversified advertiser base across thousands of local and national clients [1]

**Digital Growth Engine**
- $90.9M of the $180.8M FY2025 capex budget was devoted to digital technology, representing ~50.3% of total capex [1]
- Programmatic out-of-home currently represents approximately **2%** of existing outdoor business, described by management as a "growth area" [1]
- FY2026 projected capex: approximately $186M [1]

**Operating Model**
Lamar's hybrid model — centralized accounting/financial control with decentralized local management accountability — is designed for both margin discipline and market responsiveness [1].
