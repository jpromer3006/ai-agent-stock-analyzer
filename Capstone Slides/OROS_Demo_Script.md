# OROS Live Demo Script
### Recording guide, one script per tab · ~9 minutes total
*Record with Cmd + Shift + 5, microphone ON, Do Not Disturb ON.*

---

## Before you hit record (2 minutes of prep, not on camera)

1. Double-click **Launch.command** (local only, no tunnel needed).
2. Confirm the morning cloud run already happened (report email in your inbox). The app then shows today's data everywhere with zero waiting.
3. Have today's emailed PDF report open in a Mail or browser tab.
4. Have the OROS Market Desk YouTube channel open in another tab.
5. Do NOT plan to press Scan Now or render a video live; both take minutes. The script works entirely from cached results and points at the finished products instead.

---

## COLD OPEN (30 seconds, on the Stage Scan tab)

> "This is OROS, an autonomous multi agent stock analysis platform I built and presented as my Fordham capstone. Every weekday morning, with no human intervention, it scans five hundred liquid stocks, classifies each one into a market stage, builds a simulated portfolio with strict risk rules, emails me a research report, and broadcasts its own market show to YouTube. In the evening it comes back, grades its own morning call, and journals what it learned. Everything you're about to see was produced by the system itself. Let me walk you through the four rooms of the house."

---

## TAB 1 · 📈 STAGE SCAN (2 minutes 30)

**Point at the green regime banner.**
> "The first thing OROS checks every day is the whole market's stage, before any single stock. Today SPY is in Stage 2, an advancing phase, above a rising 30 week moving average with a momentum tailwind. That sets the playbook: in a bull regime the system favors Stage 2 breakouts. In a bear regime it would flip to capital preservation."

**Point at the OROS Scan panel with the four percentage cards.**
> "Here's this morning's scan: roughly five hundred names, every one graded into one of four stages, which the system treats as the market's seasons. Basing is spring, quiet accumulation. Advancing is summer, the only stage the system buys. Topping is autumn, distribution. Declining is winter, stay out. Today the market reads about half Advancing. This exact split is journaled, and the same numbers appear in the emailed report and tonight's broadcast. One record, every surface."

**Click one stage chart button, for example See the Advancing chart.**
> "Each grade is backed by the actual chart: price against the 30 week moving average, with volume. This is Stan Weinstein's stage analysis framework, implemented as deterministic rules, not a language model guessing. The same input always produces the same grade."

**Scroll to Confirmed BUYs.**
> "Out of five hundred names, only a handful clear every gate: price above a rising 30 week average, a volume expansion at least one and a half times baseline, and relative strength beating the S&P. The funnel is intentionally brutal. Most days most stocks fail it."

**Point at the ad hoc ticker box in the sidebar.**
> "And if I want a second opinion on any ticker, I type it here and it gets graded on the spot with the same rules."

---

## TAB 2 · 💼 PORTFOLIO (2 minutes 30)

**Point at the Robo-Advisor banner.**
> "This tab is the robo-advisor. It takes the scan and turns it into an actual simulated portfolio, regime aware and multi strategy."

**Open the Playbook checkboxes.**
> "OROS doesn't follow one book. It carries several strategy traditions as interchangeable playbooks: stage analysis, momentum in the style of Minervini, growth in the style of O'Neil, and value. In autonomous mode the system picks the playbook itself, based on its own forward scoreboard of which strategy has actually been working."

**Point at the risk settings / position sizing.**
> "Risk is engineered, never improvised: one and a quarter percent of the account risked per name, a six percent total risk wallet, sector caps, earnings blackouts, and a graduated throttle that cuts position size to a quarter when the system's own recent record turns negative. And one rule above all: the language model never sizes a trade. Sizing is pure arithmetic."

**Show the journaled build / holdings table.**
> "Here's the current book. Every position shows entry, stop, and the reasoning. This book was built by the cloud this morning; I only watched."

**Show the benchmark race chart.**
> "And here's the honest scoreboard, three equity lines from the same hundred thousand simulated dollars: the live self rebuilding book in blue, the first portfolio frozen on day one in green, and the S&P 500 in gray. The race runs to today, append only, nothing restated. As of this recording the live book leads the index."

**Point at the Email me today's report button (do not press unless you want the 30 second wait).**
> "One button regenerates and emails me the full PDF report on demand, the same artifact the autonomous run produces."

---

## TAB 3 · 🔬 RESEARCH (1 minute 45)

**Open The Historian dossier for one ticker you like today.**
> "This is The Historian. For any single stock it assembles a decision dossier: a ten year weekly chart with graded resistance zones, the current stage card, and a verdict band with a confidence reading."

**Point at the trade setup block.**
> "If there's a trade, it's spelled out completely: entry, stop, target, and the risk reward ratio. If risk reward is poor, the dossier says so plainly and the answer is don't take it."

**Show the deep memo / chat briefly.**
> "For fundamentals, a team of eighteen sector specialist agents, a REIT specialist for REITs, a bank specialist for banks, pulls structured financials straight from SEC filings and writes a cited research memo. Thirty one agents in total work this desk: orchestrators, a scanner, a classifier, the specialist bench, a chartist, a sentiment analyst, and the assistants. Each one owns its own tools."

---

## TAB 4 · 📺 YOUTUBE NEWS (1 minute 30)

**Click Load today's rundown.**
> "This might be my favorite part: transparency as a product feature. Every day OROS writes, voices, and publishes its own market show. Here's today's rundown: the cold open, the market open, the top pick with its chart and trade plan, the day's rotation, the actual holdings, and the race."

**Scroll the scene scripts.**
> "Every line of narration is editable before air, and one disclosure is enforced in code: the system proposes and journals only, it never trades, and this is education, not financial advice. No edit can remove that."

**Point at Render & publish in one click, then switch to the YouTube channel tab in Chrome.**
> "One click renders the scenes, voices them, assembles the video, and uploads it to the channel. Here's the channel with the daily episodes, and tonight the Closing Bell edition will come back, put the morning's pick on the chart, and grade it: triggered or not, right or wrong, with numbers. The system holds itself publicly accountable, win or lose."

---

## CLOSE (30 seconds, anywhere)

> "So that's OROS: a hybrid autonomous system. The cloud runs the desk every morning and evening on its own; I can step in manually any time, and every action, human or agent, lands in the same append only journal. It navigates volatility with regime awareness, democratizes institutional grade strategy, scales one analyst's discipline into thirty one agents, and replaces legacy black boxes with a system that shows its work in public every single day. Everything is simulated paper trading, educational, not financial advice. Thanks for watching."
